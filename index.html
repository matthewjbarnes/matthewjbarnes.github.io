CTO · Legal AI SaaS, Melbourne

Plexus: rebuilding delivery around AI

2025–

Plexus is a legal AI platform used by in-house legal teams at large enterprises, running on a fleet of roughly 90 microservices. My brief as CTO has had two halves: change how the company builds software, and build the agent platform underneath its AI products.

  • Put AI into the delivery process itself, not just coding assistants handed out at the edges. Engineering velocity doubled as a result, measured before and after.
  • Designed and built the agent platform on GCP with a team of two: a managed agent fleet, versioning with immutable workflow snapshots, multi-tenancy, and a simulation mode so agent behaviour can be tested before it gets anywhere near a customer. The simulation requirement was there from day one, which shaped everything else.
  • Own security for the business. Led a major security uplift; the company is ISO 27001 certified.

Drawboard: co-founder, Startmate to Microsoft

2013–18

Founder

CardLoft: shipping solo with an agent toolchain

2026–

CardLoft is a live product I design, build and run on my own: a web app for AFL and NRL trading card collectors, with collection tracking, AI card identification and valuation, and marketplace integration. I include it because it shows the delivery model taken to its logical end. One person, a set of agents, production software.

  • Next.js PWA on Firebase and GCP. The photo intake identifies and values every card on a binder page from a single shot.
  • The eBay integration has two front doors on a shared core: a REST surface for the app and an MCP surface for AI agents, with an edge-hosted OAuth broker handling authorisation.
  • Content is produced by a pipeline of agents handling discovery, research, writing and review, with me signing off before anything publishes.
